# Field travel and expense policy

**Owner:** Finance Operations
**Classification:** General Internal
**Effective:** 1 October 2026

## Purpose

This short policy explains routine expenses for approved business travel. Employees should choose reasonable options, retain receipts, and submit an expense report within ten calendar days after returning.

## Local transport

Employees may claim a standard taxi, rideshare, metro, or airport shuttle when travelling to or from an airport for an approved business trip. The trip must be itemised in the expense report. Premium ride categories are not reimbursable unless an accessibility need or a late-night safety requirement is recorded by the employee's manager.

## Meals and receipts

The daily meal allowance is INR 1,800 for domestic travel. Itemised receipts are required for individual expenses over INR 1,000. Alcohol and personal entertainment are not business expenses.

## Approval

The employee's line manager approves expense reports. Finance Operations reviews claims that exceed policy limits or lack the required receipts.
