# Employee Leave Policy
Policy HR-101. Synthetic demonstration policy, effective 1 October 2026.

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees may carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days into the next year. Carry-forward days expire on 31 March. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the employee portal. The reporting manager approves annual leave requests.
